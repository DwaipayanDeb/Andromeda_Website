Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

1. Overview: Local Architecture & User Privacy

Andromeda Writer is committed to protecting your intellectual property, research manuscripts, and personal privacy. Andromeda Writer is engineered with a strict local-first foundation: document compilation, live PDF rendering, equation building, and SyncTeX operate 100% on your local machine with zero document inspection or remote telemetry attached to your private files.

2. Information We Collect

When using our licensing portal (payment.andromedawriter.com) or desktop application, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address when you authenticate via Google Sign-In or direct email registration.
  • Subscription Status: Active license tier (Basic, Pro, or Premium), active device verification tokens, and expiration dates to synchronize licensed features to your desktop application.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure OAuth tokens generated by Google/Firebase for identity verification.
  • Anonymous LaTeX Syntax Telemetry: To improve syntax coverage and code autocompletion, the desktop app may anonymously log searched missing LaTeX command strings. No user IDs, email addresses, project code, or document content are ever attached to this telemetry.

3. AI Enhancements & Optional Connected Services

For features utilizing artificial intelligence ("AI Enhancements", powered by third-party large language models including Google Gemini):

  • Prompt Transmission: When you explicitly request AI drafting, mathematical formula derivation, or syntax generation, the specific prompt inputs and selected text snippets are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS to Google Gemini API servers to generate the completion.
  • Model Training Exemption: According to API data protection standards, user prompt data submitted through official developer APIs is not retained to train or fine-tune public foundation models.
  • Third-Party Cloud Liability Disclaimer: While transmissions use industry-standard encryption, Andromeda Writer and its developers shall NOT be held responsible or liable for any data loss, service disruptions, latency, or security breaches originating on third-party cloud infrastructure or external AI provider servers (such as Google Cloud).

4. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • We do not access, inspect, store, or transmit your private LaTeX source code, research papers, equations, bibliographies, or PDF files during standard offline editing and compilation.
  • We do not store credit card or bank payment credentials on our servers. All transactions are processed securely through PCI-compliant Merchant of Record gateways (Lemon Squeezy for global transactions, Razorpay for domestic transactions).
  • We do not sell or lease your personal information to third parties or advertising networks.

5. How We Use Information

We use collected account details solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity across authorized client devices.
  • Enable Pro and Premium features on your desktop editor.
  • Send critical account notices, such as password reset links or license receipts.
  • Expand offline LaTeX autocomplete databases using anonymous command aggregation.

6. Data Security & Retention

Authentication and telemetry records are stored on Google Cloud Firebase with 256-bit SSL encryption in transit and at rest. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting our support team.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data protection, please contact us at: contact@andromedawriter.com.
