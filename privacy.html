Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

1. Overview & Offline-First Principle

Andromeda Writer is committed to protecting your intellectual property, research data, and personal privacy. Andromeda Writer is engineered with an offline-first architecture: document compilation, live PDF rendering, equation building, and SyncTeX operate entirely on your local machine with zero telemetry attached to your document contents.

2. Information We Collect

When using our licensing and authentication portal (payment.andromedawriter.com) or desktop application, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address when you authenticate via Google Sign-In or direct email registration.
  • Subscription Status: Active license tier (Basic, Pro, or Premium), active device tokens, and expiration dates to synchronize licensed features to your desktop application.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure OAuth tokens generated by Google/Firebase for identity verification.
  • Anonymous LaTeX Syntax Telemetry: To improve syntax coverage and code autocompletion, the desktop app may anonymously log searched missing LaTeX command strings. No user IDs, email addresses, project code, or document content are ever attached to this telemetry.

3. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • We do not access, inspect, store, or transmit your LaTeX source code, research papers, equations, bibliographies, or PDF files.
  • We do not store credit card or bank payment credentials on our servers. All payments are securely processed through PCI-compliant Merchant of Record gateways (Lemon Squeezy for global transactions, Razorpay for domestic transactions).
  • We do not sell or lease your personal information to third parties or advertising networks.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected account details solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity across authorized client devices.
  • Enable Pro and Premium features on your desktop editor.
  • Send critical account notices, such as password reset links or license receipts.
  • Expand offline LaTeX autocomplete databases using anonymous command aggregation.

5. Data Security & Retention

Authentication and telemetry records are stored on Google Cloud Firebase with 256-bit SSL encryption in transit and at rest. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting our support team.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data protection, please contact us at: contact@andromedawriter.com.
