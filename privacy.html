Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Overview & Offline-First Principle

Andromeda Writer is committed to protecting your intellectual property, research data, and personal privacy. Andromeda Writer is engineered with an offline-first architecture: document compilation, live PDF rendering, and SyncTeX operate entirely on your local machine with zero telemetry attached to your document contents.

2. Information We Collect

When using our online authentication and licensing portal (payment.andromedawriter.com), we may collect:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address when you authenticate via Google Sign-In or direct email registration.
  • Subscription Status: Active license tier (Basic, Pro, or Premium) and expiration dates to synchronize paid features to your desktop application.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure OAuth tokens generated by Google/Firebase for identity verification.

3. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • We do not access, store, or transmit your LaTeX source code, equations, bibliographies, or PDF files.
  • We do not store credit card or payment credentials. All payments are processed through PCI-compliant Merchant of Record gateways (e.g. Lemon Squeezy).

4. How We Use Information

We use collected account details solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity across authorized client devices.
  • Enable Pro and Premium features on your desktop editor.
  • Send critical account notices, such as password reset links or license receipts.

5. Data Security & Retention

Authentication records are stored on Google Cloud Firebase with 256-bit SSL encryption in transit and rest. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting our support team.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@andromedawriter.com.
