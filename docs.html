Getting Started

Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Andromeda Writer. This guide covers how to download, launch, and configure your local compilation environment in under two minutes.

Andromeda is built from the ground up as a native desktop application. Core editing and LaTeX compilation operate completely offline on your local machine with zero mandatory cloud dependencies.

💡 Instant First Launch

On first startup, Andromeda automatically inspects your system PATH to locate existing LaTeX binaries (such as TeX Live, MiKTeX, or MacTeX). If detected, compilation is ready out-of-the-box.

Toolchain

TeX Engine Auto-Detection & Config

Andromeda supports multiple LaTeX compilers including pdflatex, xelatex, lualatex, and latexmk.

Automatic Discovery Strategy

During initialization, Andromeda queries the OS environment:

  • Windows: Checks standard registry keys and paths like C:\texlive\2026\bin\windows and C:\Program Files\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64.
  • Linux: Resolves system binary symlinks in /usr/bin/pdflatex, /usr/local/texlive/2026/bin/x86_64-linux.

Configuring Custom Binary Paths

If you use a custom or portable TeX distribution, you can specify the binary path directly in settings.json:

settings.json JSON 
{
  "tex_path": "C:\\texlive\\2026\\bin\\windows",
  "default_engine": "pdflatex",
  "auto_compile_on_save": true,
  "synctex_enabled": true
}
Workflow

Creating & Compiling Your First Document

Create academic articles, conference papers, and slide decks effortlessly with instant PDF split-view.

Press Ctrl + B (or F5) to trigger immediate compilation. The background worker compiles your document and automatically refreshes the PDF canvas without resetting your scroll position.

paper.tex LaTeX 
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{amsmath}

\title{My First Andromeda Document}
\author{Your Name}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\section{Introduction}
Andromeda provides instantaneous rendering for inline formulas like $E = mc^2$ 
and multi-line equations:

\begin{equation}
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi}
\end{equation}

\end{document}
Editor Features

Monaco Editor Capabilities

Powered by the industry standard Monaco Engine (the core of VS Code).

Enjoy modern editor capabilities including multi-cursor editing (Alt + Click), automatic bracket closing, intelligent code folding, syntax color customization, and live error underlines parsed directly from LaTeX log files.

Navigation

SyncTeX Bidirectional Jump

Never lose your place in 50-page manuscripts with real-time coordinate synchronization.

Direct Jump (Editor → PDF)

Place your cursor on any line in the code editor and press Ctrl + Click to highlight the corresponding paragraph in the PDF preview.

Reverse Jump (PDF → Editor)

Double click any word or equation in the PDF preview canvas to scroll the Monaco code editor directly to that exact line number.

Math Preview

Live Math Hover Tooltip

Instant visual confirmation of complex LaTeX equations before compiling.

Hover your mouse pointer over any $...$ or \begin{equation} block. Andromeda's embedded KaTeX sub-engine instantly generates a rendered mathematical tooltip above the cursor, ensuring syntax errors are caught before running the full compiler.

AI Directives

AI Chatbot Directives & Keywords

Supercharge your research and manuscript preparation using Andromeda's specialized Chatbot keywords. Directives allow you to surgically target specific files, trigger LaTeX error correction, cite local references, and discover online scholarly papers.

💡 Context Understanding & Keyword Clarity

The Andromeda AI Chatbot is intelligently designed to understand full context and intent directly from your natural language prompt. However, using these explicit @ keywords brings extra clarity to the chatbot, eliminating ambiguity and ensuring the exact target files, diagnostic engines, and citation workflows are invoked with maximum precision.

Note: The AI Chatbot agent and smart editing directives are available exclusively to Pro and Premium subscribers.

Type these keywords directly into the AI Chatbot input to invoke specialized engines and tailored workflows:

Directive Keyword Tier Required Core Functionality Review Mode
@thisdoc Pro & Premium Directly scopes edits and actions to the active document currently open in the editor Active File Diff
@<filename> Pro & Premium Points chatbot to any file (.tex, .bib, .csv, .txt, images, etc.) in the workspace Target File Diff
@latexerrors Pro & Premium LaTeX Engine error diagnosis, intent-preserving repair & log parsing Side-by-side Diff
@cite Pro & Premium Extracts references from local PDF/files, appends to BibTeX/thebib & cites in text Side-by-side Diff
@scholarly Premium Only Global literature discovery across Google Scholar, arXiv & Crossref Staged References

1. @thisdoc & @<filename> — Target File Directives

Pro & Premium

Explicitly scopes AI agent edits, transformations, and diagnostics to a designated file. This provides instant clarity to the chatbot, especially in large multi-file projects:

  • @thisdoc: Explicitly targets the document currently active and visible in the Monaco editor.
  • @<filename>: Can point to any file in your workspace — including LaTeX files (@main.tex, @chapter2.tex), bibliography files (@references.bib), tabular data (@results.csv), outline notes (@notes.txt), or illustrations. The chatbot reads the specified file context and targets proposed edits directly to it.

Usage Examples:

@thisdoc check formatting of table 1 and convert to booktabs
@chapter2.tex expand the proof of Theorem 3
@references.bib format all entries in IEEE style
@data.csv convert this dataset into a LaTeX tabular environment

Expected Outcome:

  • Loads the exact file context and targets proposed edits specifically to the named file.
  • Opens Monaco Diff Review Mode centered on the modified sections of that file with side-by-side Accept/Reject controls.

2. @latexerrors — Specialized LaTeX Engine Diagnostics

Pro & Premium

Directs Andromeda's specialized AI LaTeX Engine (#Latex_Engine) to read the compiler diagnostic log directly and perform surgical, intent-preserving repair of syntax errors, unclosed environments, math mode delimiters, and missing preamble packages.

⚡ Smart Compile Rule

The engine directly parses filename.log if it already exists without compiling. Andromeda initiates compilation of filename.tex only if asked to correct errors and the corresponding .log file is missing.

Usage Examples:

Correct LaTeX errors @latexerrors
@latexerrors resolve undefined control sequence \nu on line 42 in article.tex
Please fix compilation issues @latexerrors

Expected Outcome:

  • Error causing lines are identified and cataloged in .andromeda/.agent/latex_errors.json.
  • Author intent is strictly preserved (e.g. \nu wrapped into $\nu$, \textit{R_cm} inside equations repaired to R_{cm}).
  • Proposed resolution is staged directly to Monaco Diff Review Mode with side-by-side Accept/Reject controls.

3. @cite — Local Reference Extraction & In-Text Citation

Pro & Premium

Instructs the agent to extract reference metadata from local files (PDFs, BibTeX, or text files) stored in your project root or subfolders. The agent automatically executes two synchronized operations:

  1. Bibliography Appending: Appends the formatted citation to your references.bib file or under \begin{thebibliography} (whichever format your target LaTeX document uses).
  2. In-Text Citation Placement: Analyzes the document body and inserts ~\cite{cite_key} in the proper contextual location (e.g., in the Introduction, Related Work, or a user-specified section).
🔒 Privacy & Local Isolation

@cite operates entirely on your local project assets and does not consume external literature quota or invoke ai_cite_engine.

Usage Examples:

@cite sample_paper.pdf in section 2
@cite papers/quantum_study.pdf at the end of Introduction
@cite references.bib

Expected Outcome:

  • Extracts publication title, authors, year, and a clean cite key (e.g. quantum2024).
  • Inserts ~\cite{quantum2024} into the active .tex file at the specified or most contextually relevant position.
  • Appends the BibTeX entry to references.bib or \bibitem before \end{thebibliography}.
  • Stages changes in Monaco Diff Review Mode for side-by-side inspection and acceptance.

4. @scholarly — Academic Literature & Paper Search

Premium Only

Activates Andromeda's specialized Citation Engine (ai_cite_engine) to search online academic literature across Google Scholar, arXiv, PubMed, and Crossref.

Usage Examples:

@scholarly search recent peer-reviewed articles on quantum error correction
@scholarly find benchmark literature on transformer attention mechanisms
@scholarly "Graph Neural Networks for Drug Discovery"

Expected Outcome:

  • Retrieves peer-reviewed publications with verified DOIs, author lists, and publication venues.
  • Generates standard BibTeX entries formatted for direct inclusion into your project references.
  • Non-premium accounts receive an upgrade prompt: "Please upgrade to Premium to use this feature".

Support & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Q: How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

You can cancel your subscription at any time without hassle or penalties. The cancellation process depends on your payment method:

International Subscriptions (Lemon Squeezy — $ USD)

  • Via Email Receipt: Open any email receipt sent to you by Lemon Squeezy upon subscribing or renewal, and click the "Manage Subscription" or "View Order" link.
  • Via Customer Portal: Log in directly to the Lemon Squeezy Customer Billing Portal with your checkout email to cancel with 1 click.
  • Via Andromeda Web Portal: Sign in at user.andromedawriter.com/account.html and click "Manage / Cancel Subscription".

Domestic Indian Subscriptions (Razorpay — ₹ INR)

  • Directly in your UPI App (Fastest): Open your UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM) → navigate to Settings > UPI AutoPay / Mandates → select Andromeda Writer → tap "Cancel AutoPay / Revoke Mandate". Future debits will stop immediately.
  • Via Razorpay Notifications: Use the direct subscription management link delivered in Razorpay's SMS/email confirmation.
  • Via Support: Send an email to contact@andromedawriter.com or use the Contact Form with your account email and our team will assist within 24 hours.
Q: What happens if I cancel during the 7-day free trial?

If you cancel anytime during your 7-day trial period, your payment method will not be charged ($0.00 / ₹0). You will retain full access to all Pro or Premium features for the entire remainder of the 7 days. Once the 7 days conclude, your account will automatically transition to the free Basic tier with zero obligation.

Q: How do I get my license on a new computer?

Just download Andromeda Writer on your new device and log in with your registered account email. Your subscription is cloud-synced, allowing you to use your license seamlessly across your personal devices.

Q: Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your monthly subscription anytime with zero penalty directly from your account settings at user.andromedawriter.com/account.html.

Q: "pdflatex not found" error during first compile?

Ensure you have a working TeX distribution (such as TeX Live or MiKTeX) installed. Open Andromeda Settings (Ctrl + ,) and enter the full directory path to your pdflatex.exe or pdflatex binary.

Q: Can I use custom .cls or .sty journal templates?

Yes! Place your custom .cls, .sty, and .bib files directly into your project folder. Andromeda preserves the working directory during compilation so all local assets are linked smoothly.
