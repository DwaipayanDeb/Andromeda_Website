Andromeda Writer combines the power of VS Code's editor engine with the speed of a local TeX pipeline.

100% Offline & Private

Your research, unreleased equations, and confidential manuscripts never leave your machine. No mandatory accounts, telemetry, or third-party servers.

Monaco Editor Core

Equipped with VS Code's Monaco engine: multi-cursor editing, bracket colorization, intelligent LaTeX snippets, and automated BibTeX citation completion.

SyncTeX Bidirectional Jump

Click any sentence or math equation in the rendered PDF to instantly jump to the exact line in your `.tex` source code, and vice-versa.

Live Math Hover Preview

Hover over complex matrices or integrals in the code editor to view a crisp, rendered visual preview instantly without having to recompile the document.

Smart TeX Auto-Detection

Automatically discovers your existing TeX Live, MiKTeX, or MacTeX installation across system paths and configures the build toolchain seamlessly.

AI Academic Assistant

Integrated AI writing assistant capable of fixing compilation errors, formatting tables, suggesting bibliography entries, and proofreading mathematical text.
